## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.21

The biggest update in a while: safety controls, a plain list of what the AI did (with Undo), a notes vault the AI keeps up to date, and fixes for the AI pausing in the middle of long jobs.

### ✨ What's new

- Read-only mode: the AI can look but not change anything. It also asks before deleting files.
- What happened: a simple list of what the AI did, with an Undo button.
- Vault: notes the AI reads and updates itself.
- Save a setup for each site, share skills with a link, back up your settings.
- Ask the AI how any PlazCode feature works.
- A new look for command cards on every AI site.

### 🛠️ What's fixed

- Claude no longer stops at random in the middle of a long run of tools.
- If a result never reached the chat box, PlazCode now tries again, or tells you right away instead of waiting a minute.
- Leftover empty boxes after commands on Claude are gone.
- The bar no longer says "AgentScript is offline" while the app is running.
- Fix connection button no longer freezes some sites. Notion retries files it rejects.
- The popup to continue your last project is cleaner and skips chats that only had the startup message.

### 🧪 What was checked

- Full tests passed on Windows, Mac and Linux, including the Mac app.
- On Claude, two live runs of 15 tools in a row finished with no pause. Other sites were tested with practice pages, not live.
- The Windows app is unsigned and the Mac app is not notarized, as before. Your computer may ask you to confirm opening it.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.21.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.21.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.21.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.

Test details are included in VALIDATION.txt.
