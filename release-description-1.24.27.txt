✨ **PlazCode 1.24.27**
***Your own profile and picture, approval choices, and faster, steadier tool calls.***

***✨ What's new***
- Profiles with JPEG, PNG or animated GIF pictures, remembered login, and settings/private skills that sync. Files, memory and access stay local.
- Pick Ask, Approve for me or Bypass approval in the bar or Settings.
- Native Linux app opens in your browser.

***🛠️ What's fixed***
- Claude and most sites run a command about 1 second after it finishes, not up to 12 (DeepSeek keeps safe pacing).
- Helper results carry their own names; unknown-command errors suggest real ones; file sizes no longer show TB.
- The bridge no longer drops silently on status checks.
- The bar follows your chat box; every effort option stays reachable.
- DeepSeek sends share one queue, 8–9.8 seconds apart.
- Mac recovers a stuck port; startup plays once and checks faster.

***🧪 What was checked***
- Windows, Mac and Linux builds, both browsers, updates and Mac startup passed on practice pages. Live AI speed and real accounts were not tested.
- Random disconnects from one tester are not fully explained yet.
- Windows is unsigned and Mac is not notarized, as before.

***📥 How to update***
- Open PlazCode, or choose Updates → Update now.
- Manual downloads: PlazCode-1.24.27.zip for Windows, PlazCode-macOS-1.24.27.zip for Mac, or PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.27.zip for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
