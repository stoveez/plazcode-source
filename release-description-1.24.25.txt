✨ **PlazCode 1.24.25**
***Screenshots, approvals and updates:*** clearer results and fewer repeated prompts.

***✨ What's new***
- The Updates page shows active browser extension versions and warns when the app and extension do not match.

***🛠️ What's fixed***
- Screenshot previews can display even when an AI site blocks image URLs; unreadable captures show an error.
- Approve all for this task now skips repeats of that command in Plan Mode.
- Known outdated versions stay red when a later update check fails.
- Creation previews explain missing app support, large requests and invalid replies instead of a JSON parsing error.

***🧪 What was checked***
- Full Windows, Mac and Linux tests, including the packaged Mac app; screenshot pixels checked in Chromium and Firefox. Small creation drafts checked through the real desktop endpoint.
- The affected computer and real Studio captures were not tested live. Updating only the EXE cannot update a separate extension folder.
- The Windows app is unsigned and the Mac app is not notarized, as before.

***📥 How to update***
- Open PlazCode, or choose Updates → Update now.
- Manual downloads: PlazCode-1.24.25.zip for Windows, PlazCode-macOS-1.24.25.zip for Mac, or PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.25.zip for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
