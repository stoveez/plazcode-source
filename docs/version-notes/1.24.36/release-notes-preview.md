✨ **PlazCode 1.24.36**
***A quick follow-up: your taskbar works while the AI controls the screen, and Linux users can connect Roblox Studio through TuxBlox.***

***✨ What's new***
- Linux: Roblox Studio works through TuxBlox as well as Vinegar. No fake flatpak or edited menu entry needed.
- Linux: PlazCode uses whichever one runs your open Studio (TuxBlox first) and shows the helper's own message if it cannot start.
- Linux: a helper that stops at once is retried less often, and a slow first start gets up to a minute.

***🛠️ What's fixed***
- Windows: the taskbar no longer disappears while the AI controls your screen. It shows when you move the mouse down, appears in screenshots and works while paused.

***🧪 What was checked***
- Automated tests on Windows, Mac and Linux, including the real Linux app with a stand-in TuxBlox. Not yet tried on a real Linux PC with TuxBlox, or with a real taskbar. Report anything odd.
- Windows is unsigned and Mac is not notarized, as before.

***📥 How to update***
- Open PlazCode, or choose Updates → Update now.
- Manual downloads: PlazCode-1.24.36.zip for Windows, PlazCode-macOS-1.24.36.zip for Mac, PlazCode-Linux-1.24.36.tar.gz for Linux, or PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.36.zip for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
