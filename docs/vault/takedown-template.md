# Takedown preparation template
Updated: 2026-11-01. Owner completes and submits; nothing is sent automatically. Not legal advice.

Use only after reviewing [licence and branding findings](license-and-branding-review.md). GPL-permitted copying/modification alone is not a takedown basis. The owner must establish the relevant rights and consider the recipient's licence and lawful uses.

## Evidence checklist
- Original material and the rights holder: `[specific files/artwork/text, ownership evidence, applicable licence]`.
- Official distribution: https://github.com/stoveez/PlazCode/releases and `[specific version/asset link]`.
- Alleged copy: `[exact repository/file/download/page URLs]`.
- Comparison: `[matching protected expression, retained or removed notices, misleading official-branding statements]`.
- Optional release mark: `[mark and version from release-marks.md, signed-manifest/file hashes]`. A matching mark is supporting provenance evidence, not proof of infringement.
- Licence assessment: `[why this particular use exceeds the actual grant; assess permission, exceptions and fair use]`.
- Requested action limited to the identified issue: `[remove specified infringing material or correct misleading affiliation]`.

## Submission destinations
- GitHub: its official DMCA process at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/guide-to-submitting-a-dmca-takedown-notice . Read requirements before submitting.
- Other hosts: the actual host's published copyright/abuse or affiliation-reporting procedure. Confirm the host and exact URLs; do not send bulk notices or contact unrelated parties.
