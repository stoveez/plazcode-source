✨ **PlazCode 1.24.33**
***PlazCode can now work in your tabs and apps while you watch, plus easy app connections and a fresh look.***

***✨ What's new***
- Ask PlazCode (Alt+P, or the bar button in AgentScript): a side chat that sees your tab or screen and can control it if you allow it.
- When the AI works in a tab or app, it glows in your theme colour with PlazCode's cursor and a live preview in the chat. Move your mouse to take over.
- Apps & servers: connect Blender, Godot, Unity, Figma and Blockbench with a checklist that ticks itself off. Guided extension setup too.
- Blender works in RobloxScript too. Models reach Studio at the right size, facing the right way, with colours.
- Linux: one-line install with automatic updates: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stoveez/PlazCode/main/install-linux.sh | sh (Studio via Vinegar: experimental)
- A redesigned PlazCode bar, settings menu and toolbar popup that match the app.
- Helper agents, several commands per reply, and Mac support from macOS 10.15 (memory search needs 12).

***🛠️ What's fixed***
- Notion: commands right after a sentence run again.
- DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi and similar sites: the startup check works again.
- Commands no longer wait on the link to the app.
- AI sites' own errors no longer show as PlazCode errors.

***🧪 What was checked***
- Automated tests on Windows, Mac and Linux. Tab and screen control tested on practice pages, not on every real site or a real Mac yet. Please report anything odd.
- Windows is unsigned and Mac is not notarized, as before.

***📥 How to update***
- Open PlazCode, or choose Updates → Update now.
- Manual downloads: PlazCode-1.24.33.zip for Windows, PlazCode-macOS-1.24.33.zip for Mac, PlazCode-Linux-1.24.33.tar.gz for Linux, or PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.33.zip for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
