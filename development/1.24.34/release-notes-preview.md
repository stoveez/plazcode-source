✨ **PlazCode 1.24.34**
***Ask PlazCode runs in your own chat, a smoother app, and less AI plan usage.***

***✨ What's new***
- Ask PlazCode runs in the AI chat you are using, not a background DeepSeek chat. Replies show as they are written.
- The AI can use other desktop apps by their button names and maximize, minimize or restore windows in one step.
- Live preview can be dragged or minimized; the glow stays on while the AI works.
- Only a click or key press takes control back; moving the mouse or scrolling no longer pauses the AI.
- Less AI plan usage: shorter status lines, smaller start-up list, at most 2 commands per reply.
- Switch to turn off the PlazCode vault if you use your own notes.
- The task list folds away with PlazCode and can be dragged.
- Smoother Windows app: lighter page changes, less blur, idle animations rest.
- ClickUp support removed; the app lists 15 browser AIs + Ollama.

***🛠️ What's fixed***
- Resume no longer flips back to Pause.
- DeepSeek starts on non-English pages and on Edge.
- Gemini: cards line up, no typing lag, no false "bridge not active".
- Ask PlazCode answers no longer go missing, and it shows thinking dots.
- ChatGPT keeps working when its window is not in front.
- plazcode_clarify: your own "Other" answer can be sent.
- Mac app window works on Catalina again.
- Settings language list no longer hides behind sections.

***🧪 What was checked***
- Automated tests on Windows, Mac and Linux; Gemini checked live, most others not yet. Report anything odd.
- Windows is unsigned and Mac is not notarized, as before.

***📥 How to update***
- Open PlazCode, or choose Updates → Update now.
- Manual downloads: PlazCode-1.24.34.zip for Windows, PlazCode-macOS-1.24.34.zip for Mac, PlazCode-Linux-1.24.34.tar.gz for Linux, or PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.34.zip for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
