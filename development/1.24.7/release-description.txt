## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.7

Give your skills a name, or let PlazCode name them for you. Learning and library options now have clearer spacing.

### ✏️ Name your skills

- **Choose a name:** a new optional name box sits above Skill instructions.
- **Automatic naming:** leave the name blank and PlazCode makes a short name from what you wrote. You can rename saved skills too.

### 🎨 Clearer Skills layout

- **More breathing room:** Learning and library options is separated from the editor. Opening it keeps its buttons and settings from crowding each other.
- **Keep your workflow:** saving, project scope, importing JSON or text, and exporting JSON work as before.

### 📝 Easier release notes

- **Plain language:** update descriptions explain what changed and how it helps, without requiring scripting knowledge.

### ✅ Checked before release

- Automated checks cover Skills, startup, commands, updating and Windows/Mac builds. Live AI accounts and real Studio sessions were not tested.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.7.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.7.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.7.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.

Test details are included in VALIDATION.txt.
