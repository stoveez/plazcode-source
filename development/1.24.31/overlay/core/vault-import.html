Import notes into your vault

Pick a folder of notes, for example an Obsidian vault. PlazCode copies the notes into your open vault so every AI can use them. The original folder is never changed.

Notes go into 10 Inbox/Imported <folder name>. Notes with a password or key inside, notes that already exist, empty notes, pictures and hidden folders are skipped, and listed in a report note.
