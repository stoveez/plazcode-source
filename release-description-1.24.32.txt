✨ **PlazCode 1.24.32**
***Fixes for the Notion bar, the blocked chatbox, command running and wrong Offline messages.***

***✨ What's new***
- The hidden PlazCode tab can be dragged around the screen; it returns to its place when you show the bar again.

***🛠️ What's fixed***
- Notion: the PlazCode bar no longer gets stuck out of place when another page or side panel opens.
- Notion: command cards attach to the code block, never to the reply's text.
- Notion: custom agent chats are recognised.
- The chatbox is no longer blocked for seconds after the AI finishes replying.
- Only the real command block runs; examples in text, plaintext or inline code are ignored.
- The app and bar no longer wrongly show Offline or Check unavailable while connected.

***🧪 What was checked***
- Automated tests on Windows, Mac and Linux, and on practice pages. Not tried live on every AI site or in Studio; please report anything odd.
- The Windows app is unsigned and the Mac app is not notarized, as before. Your computer may ask you to confirm opening it.

***📥 How to update***
- Open PlazCode, or choose Updates → Update now.
- Manual downloads: PlazCode-1.24.32.zip for Windows, PlazCode-macOS-1.24.32.zip for Mac, or PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.32.zip for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
