**Vault memory** is a folder of linked notes the AI reads and updates while it works, so it does not forget your project between chats.

## See your notes
Open **Settings > Memory** and press the vault button. A viewer window opens with your notes and how they link together.

## Turn it off
The AI writes notes automatically. You can switch that off in the same Memory settings.

## Reviewing work
For image-based jobs the AI can ask a separate critic chat to score the result and redo it until it passes.
