Open **Settings > Appearance**.

![The Appearance page with color theme swatches](appearance.jpg)

## Color themes
Choose any swatch. Use **Add Custom Theme** to pick your own color.

## Make it yours
Change the font, corner roundness, border thickness and color, turn on the rainbow border, or show a faint grid.

## Wallpaper
Press **Choose file** and pick a picture, a GIF, or a short MP4 or WebM video. Use **Wallpaper strength**, **Panel blur** and **Panel opacity** to make cards easy to read on top of it.

> Looks are saved on this computer. Themes also color the PlazCode bar in your browser; custom colors and wallpapers only change the app.
