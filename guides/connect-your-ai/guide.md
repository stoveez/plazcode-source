PlazCode turns an AI chat website into an agent. It works with the AI account you already have.

## Pick an AI
On the Overview, choose one of the supported AIs. The app opens it in your browser.

## Look for the bar
When the chat page loads, the PlazCode bar appears. If it does not, see [PlazCode bar is missing](troubleshooting-bar-missing).

## Start the agent
Press **Start Agent** in the bar, or on the Overview. PlazCode begins sending your requests to your AI and carrying out the answers.

> Existing accounts or subscriptions may be needed on the AI's own website.
