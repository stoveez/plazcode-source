## Choose an engine
On the Overview, the Agent section has an engine switch:
- **RobloxScript** edits the open Roblox game through Roblox Studio.
- **AgentScript** works with files and commands in a folder on your computer.

## Describe the job
Type your request in the AI chat as you normally would. Be specific about what you want built or changed.

## Follow along
The bar shows what the AI is doing, one step at a time. Stop at any moment with the stop button. Safety settings, such as read-only mode and confirm before deleting, are in **Settings > Safety**.
