## Automatic updates
PlazCode checks for a new version every time it starts. With **Automatic updates** on, it installs the new version for you when you are not in the middle of work.

## Update Log
Open **Home > Update Log** to see what changed in each version.

## After an update
Reload the extension in your browser, then refresh your open AI chat tabs. This keeps the app and the extension on the same version.
