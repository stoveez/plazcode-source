✨ **PlazCode 1.24.35**
***When the AI finishes a Roblox task, PlazCode now plays the game, checks the UI and has the AI fix what broke.***

***✨ What's new***
- Playtest & Fix: after a Roblox task, PlazCode plays the game for 10 s, reads the errors and has the AI fix them.
- UI check: the AI follows a UI standard and the menus it builds are checked and fixed before the playtest.
- PlazLib: ready-made Luau helpers so the AI builds faster with fewer mistakes.
- Import a whole folder of skills (or drop a folder or .zip).
- Resume or Start fresh after a tab reload or crash during a run.
- Keep my computer awake during runs (off by default).
- The Ask PlazCode panel is hidden from the AI's screen pictures.
- Automation switches like Playtest when done are in the app too.

***🛠️ What's fixed***
- DeepSeek with web search on: commands that stayed raw JSON blocks and did nothing now run.
- DeepSeek keeps your DeepThink and Search choices at Start; no more raw JSON flicker.
- "Send paused" during tool use is far rarer on ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini and Kimi.
- Old chats no longer start running by themselves.
- Every Appearance setting works, including panel opacity, blur, corners and borders.
- Settings from the extension sync to the app and back, even if the app was closed.
- Mac app layout on Catalina.

***🧪 What was checked***
- Automated tests on Windows, Mac and Linux; the Roblox helpers ran in the real Luau engine. Not yet tried in real Studio or most live AI sites. Report anything odd.
- Windows is unsigned and Mac is not notarized, as before.

***📥 How to update***
- Open PlazCode, or choose Updates → Update now.
- Manual downloads: PlazCode-1.24.35.zip for Windows, PlazCode-macOS-1.24.35.zip for Mac, PlazCode-Linux-1.24.35.tar.gz for Linux, or PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.35.zip for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
