✨ **PlazCode 1.24.22**
***Start Agent now fits free plans:*** a much shorter startup, so Claude, ClickUp and ChatGPT free plans no longer run out or refuse it.

***✨ What's new***
- A Full startup switch in the PlazCode menu (Automation), off by default, for people who want every instruction sent at once.

***🛠️ What's fixed***
- The startup is about 6,000 characters instead of about 44,000, on every AI site.
- Claude and ClickUp free plans no longer use up their limit on the startup alone.
- ChatGPT's free plan no longer refuses the startup as too long.
- On DeepSeek, PlazCode waits at least 5 seconds between its own messages and tells you once that DeepSeek may suspend accounts that use automated tools.
- The Plan and License cards are gone from the app. PlazCode is free.

***🧪 What was checked***
- Full tests passed on Windows, Mac and Linux, including the Mac app.
- The shorter startup was tested with practice pages, not yet live on each site's free plan.
- The Windows app is unsigned and the Mac app is not notarized, as before. Your computer may ask you to confirm opening it.

***📥 How to update***
- Open PlazCode, or choose Updates → Update now.
- Manual downloads: PlazCode-1.24.22.zip for Windows, PlazCode-macOS-1.24.22.zip for Mac, or PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.22.zip for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
